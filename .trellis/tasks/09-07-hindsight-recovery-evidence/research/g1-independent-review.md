Proof and diagnostics remain explicit, later rows complete, and reopen does not redispatch. The leader reproduced the failure and reported 71 passing tests across the three affected Core files. The reviewer independently ran the two-row, file-backed SQLite/reopen regression at `packages/core/src/memory/hindsight/hindsight-pending-recovery.test.ts:448`: 1 passed, 36 excluded by the selector, 2.44 s (tool chunk `023ac3`).

The real chunks-mode regression at `tests/native_results/test_review_source_fences.py:100` was RED in `a79adbde3c9d` and GREEN in `9e418bb81520`. Its unique-marker assertion checks native facts, not just the document body or receipt ordinals.    The final counter refinement was separately reviewed: `NativeRestoredParentOutcome` at `hindsight_api/engine/native_receipt_lifecycle.py:34` keeps missing historical counts explicitly null and marks the aggregate incomplete. `hindsight_api/engine/memory_engine.py:3447` preserves the ordinary native `RetainOutcomeMetadata.to_dict()` path and the original SQL JSON merge.
