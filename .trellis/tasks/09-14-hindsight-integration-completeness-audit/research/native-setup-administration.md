## Verification and negative-search scope

- Existing B3/B4/D78 records and the [bounded B6 closeout](../../08-09-memory-runtime-integration/research/b6-closeout-validation-2026-09-13.md)
  remain evidence for their named cases. They do not prove a setup wizard, managed deployment, upgrade or full recovery UI.
- Searches were bounded to production Core server/boot/memory, Shared schemas, desktop and scripts. Helper/backup/upgrade
  terms found the receipt/schema and service consumers, not a production administrative workflow. Model-field searches
  found configuration, fingerprint and secret-reference declarations, not native model-configuration application.
- Setup failure, new-Character and stale-validation conclusions are source-level findings. Reproduction and fixes belong
  to subsequent authorized implementation, with the concrete acceptance cases above.
- Secret storage code is credited through the [multi-endpoint report](multi-endpoint-memory.md), ME-04/V4. Real Windows
  helper/keyring and headless/packaged secret acceptance remain separate environment checks, not newly passed tests.
- No source conclusion depends on the old R1 new-Bank/full-copy proposal. Ordinary re-entry retains accepted verified
  baseline/mapping/change reuse; independent-Core tenancy remains a different, unsupported/unverified scenario.
