source. This is source/evidence review, not a new runtime or model test. Existing test reports retain their original
dates and fixture limits. Full project paths are relative to H:/TakiAgent. Shorthand memory/ and db/ are under
packages/core/src/; bare canonical/local filenames are under its memory/ directory, and bare hindsight-*.ts filenames
