## Reviewed changes

- Core now verifies all-resource revision/payload/definition progress and original receipt journal movement,
  while permitting legitimate later current-object progress. Adjacent full-registry tests include 058 and
  prove physical cleanup, historical reopen and outer-COMMIT selection/default rollback.
- Shared and Native agree on bounded structural values, complete triggers and label shape. Native retains
  authoritative croniter compilation; no new dependency or narrower public cron grammar was introduced.
- Optional task-local provider ownership is checked after actual permit/retry waits and before later model,
  cache and embedding submissions. Copied contexts are revoked on owner exit. Final transaction guards and
  original child semantic results remain authoritative; submitted external work is not promised exactly once.
- Native backup/FK and transition auxiliary inventories include receipts, children and refresh results.
  All-absent old schemas preserve their previous witness; partial installations refuse. A no-op receipt
  changes the complete witness even when the ordinary logical cursors do not move.
